# Cast-in features

## Current design: EG tub in `Type_0_v3` (Fusion, read 23 Nov 2026)

The moving-gantry design has one casting, the EG tub. Coordinates are from `Type_0_v3`: X right, Y back, Z up. The tub is X -390 to 390, Y -37 to 653, Z -15 to 107. Casting volume 38.63 L, about **88.8 kg** at 2,300 kg/m³. It is cast upside down, with the wall tops on the flat mould floor and the bars and strips bolted to it (D-204). Every insert is prepared as D-118: abraded, degreased, threads plugged from the back, and a fine resin-rich mix against it.
