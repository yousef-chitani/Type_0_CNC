# Steel box section, cut to length: suppliers near Swansea (17 Nov 2026)

Nothing is decided here. This is a phone list for the owner, who will price and collect.

**What to ask for**
- Beam: SHS 150 × 150 × 8, S355. Hot-finished (EN 10210) preferred, cold-formed (EN 10219) is fine. 890 mm, or 1 m. Weighs about 35.4 kg/m, so about 31.5 kg for 890 mm.
- For comparison: RHS 150 × 100 × 8, same length.
- Saddle: offcuts about 400 mm of 150 × 100 and 200 × 100 RHS (size not final).

Distances are rough road miles from SA2. "Not stated" means the website does not say, so ask.

**Why this order**
1. **Dyfed Steels** is the only local stockholder whose own site confirms all of it: RHS and SHS stock (S355 and S235), one-off cuts, and hot-finished on request ("If you require Hot Finished material please send us your enquiries"). Stock is cold-formed, in 7.5, 10 and 12 m lengths. On the call, ask:
   - do they sell to a private buyer, cash or card, and is there a minimum order or cut charge?
   - can they cut 890 mm (or 1 m) of 150 × 150 × 8 from a bar, and do they have hot-finished?
   - can they price 150 × 100 × 8 too, and 400 mm offcuts of 150 × 100 and 200 × 100?
   - **is the Kemys Way (Swansea) depot still open for collection?**
2. **Ellis Steel, Kemys Way**: Companies House shows Dyfed Steels took control of Ellis Steel Group on 19 Aug 2026 ([PSC record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00979006/persons-with-significant-control)). Its registered office is now Dyfed's Dafen site, and Dyfed's branch list has no Swansea branch. So the Swansea yard may be closed or folded into Dyfed. Ask Dyfed (above) before driving there.
3. **K I Metals** is the only seller whose live page shows a price for the exact beam size. The page shows the price before a length is typed. It is probably per metre (about £2.55/kg), but check in their cut-to-size box. The grade is "commonly S235JRH to EN 10219; selected sections may be S355J2H", so ask for S355 in writing. Free delivery over £150 + VAT, but "heavy/oversize exclusions" apply, so a 32 kg piece may cost extra to ship.
4. **F.H. Brundle** is the fallback trade counter if Dyfed won't serve a private buyer. It is a long drive, and its stock may stop below 8 mm wall in 150 sizes, so phone first.
5. **Merit Engineering** is a local fabricator for saddle offcuts. Its number comes from a directory.
