# Aggregates: sourcing (owner prefers collection)

The v2 castings need about **105 kg** of aggregate (118 kg of EG at 89%), plus about 15 kg for the test bricks (D-125). The blend is tuned on the test bricks (`eg-mix-test-plan.md`, blends P1–P3); coarse is about 50% (D-125).

- The 1–2 mm gap is acceptable (gap-graded). If the test bricks show the epoxy demand is too high, add 2 bags of GCL 1–3 mm (the fallback, D-125).
- **Everything must be clean (no clay or silt) and completely dry before mixing.** Damp or dusty aggregate weakens the epoxy bond.

## If local damp material is ever used
- **Coarse:** keep the largest stone under about 1/5 of the thinnest section, and use washed 2–6 mm grit around the inserts. Granite or quartz is best; limestone works but is softer.
- **Medium:** washed sharp sand, sieved to remove anything over about 2 mm.
- **Drying:** spread thinly and dry, then finish in an oven at about 100–120 °C. Check by weighing a sample before and after oven drying: there should be no weight loss.
- **Kit:** 2 mm and 5 mm sieves (not on the parts list).

## Whiting datasheet (Valentine Clays, owner-supplied)
- Moisture 0.1% ex works: use as supplied, no drying.
- d50 9 µm, top cut (d98) 110 µm, 4% retained on 53 µm, 19% below 2 µm.
- SG 2.7, 99% CaCO₃, pH 9, oil absorption 17 g/100 g, Blaine 480 m²/kg.
- **Limit to about 8–10% of the aggregate mass.** The very fine part raises viscosity and epoxy demand. Tune on test bricks.
- Paper bags: decant into a sealed tub once opened. Warm before use if stored in a damp garage.
