## Questions to ask Gravel Works
1. What is the **10 mm clean stone** and the **stone dust**: granite, limestone or something else? (Granite or quartz is best. Limestone works but is softer.)
2. Do you have a **washed grit or chippings 2–6 mm** (or 4–10 mm)?
3. Is the **sharp sand washed**, and roughly what size range (usually 0–4 mm)?
4. Do you sell **small quantities for collection** (bags or a part bulk bag): about 150 kg coarse + 100 kg sand?
5. Price for collection.

## If using local damp material
- **Coarse:** 10 mm clean stone is usable for a 180 mm base (keep the max size under about 1/5 of the thinnest section). It shifts the grading coarser, which can lower the epoxy needed. Washed 2–6 mm grit is better around the inserts.
- **Medium:** washed sharp sand, sieved to remove anything over about 2 mm.
- **Fine:** B&Q kiln-dried sand (already dry).
- **Filler:** silica flour (Potterycrafts) or granite stone dust, if it is dry and clay-free.
- **Drying:** spread thinly and dry, then finish in an oven at about 100–120 °C, or a hot day plus a few days under cover. Check by weighing a sample before and after oven drying: there should be no weight loss.
- **Kit:** 2 mm and 5 mm sieves (add to the BOM if this route is chosen).
