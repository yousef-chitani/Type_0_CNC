=== JOB 1: ONE ALIEXPRESS SESSION (do this first) ===

Log in to AliExpress, set delivery to UK.

A) Look up these 4 items. For each, write down: price with the option
   selected, shipping cost, delivery date, and the link.

   1. Engineer's square, 300 mm blade.
      Search: "engineer square 300mm DIN875"
      The page MUST say DIN 875 grade 0, 1 or 2. If no grade is stated,
      skip it.
      UK price to beat: GBP 74.40

   2. Steel shim sheets.
      Search: "steel shim sheet 0.05mm" (then 0.1mm and 0.2mm)
      Must be steel or stainless (not brass, not plastic), with at least
      0.05, 0.1 and 0.2 mm thick sheets, each about 150 x 150 mm or bigger.
      UK price to beat: GBP 58.19

   3. Bolts: 100 x M5 x 20 socket cap screws, grade 12.9.
      Search: "M5x20 socket head cap screw 12.9"
      Price to beat: GBP 11.78 (Screwfix stainless, 2 packs of 50)

   4. (Optional) Straightedge, 500 mm steel.
      Search: "steel straight edge 500mm DIN874"
      Must say DIN 874 grade 0, 1 or 2.
      Price to beat: GBP 115.07

B) Send 2 chat messages to sellers. The exact text is in
   supplier-messages.md - copy and paste it.
   - Section 1: the ballscrew seller (asks what "Guide Length" means).
     This one matters most: a wrong answer means screws you can't use.
   - Section 4: the Z motor mount seller (asks for the drawing).
   Write the date you sent each one under the message in that file,
   or just tell the next session.

C) Tidy the AliExpress basket:
   - Check the Waveshare USB-RS485 adapter photos show screw terminals
     marked A+, B- and GND.
   - Remove the 6 mm tube.
   - Check the ferrules (12 packs), the HSC8 16-6 crimper and the
     20 and 14 AWG wire are still there.
   - Add the spindle kit (S08) and the flow switch (S05), and note the
     S08 price. No radiator or fans now (a 20 L bucket instead).
