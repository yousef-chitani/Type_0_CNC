# Open questions

Roughly in the order we'll work through them.

1. Z travel and clearance under the spindle
2. Y travel: 250 mm of work plus about 100 mm extra for the tool change. Confirm the clearances
3. Rack mechanism: pneumatic slide or linear actuator, dust cover, tool-length sensor location
4. Motion parts: rail size and preload, ballscrew grade (C7 vs C5), screw diameter and lead
5. EG recipe and casting method: aggregate grading, epoxy brand, vibration table, steel inserts
6. Rail mounting surfaces: epoxy-leveled, replicated, or bolted steel/ground plates
7. Closed-loop stepper sizes (NEMA 23 vs 24), and direct drive vs belt
8. Mesa board choice (e.g. 7i96S, 7i76EU, 7i95T) and I/O count (ATC, air, sensors, VFD)
9. Spindle cooling (water or air) and VFD control (Modbus vs analog)
10. Budget split across subsystems
