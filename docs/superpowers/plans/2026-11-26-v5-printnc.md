# Type 0 V5 (PrintNC with Type 0's own parts): implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the approved V5 design into checked sizes, a chosen tall beam, a list of every part and how it is made without a shop, a clash-free Fusion model `Type_0_v5` built from a fresh copy of PrintNC, a rail-seat pour plan with its pass mark, and a priced V5 BOM with wave 1 lengths.

**Architecture:** A Python sizing script fixes every length from the work area, the 16-tool rack and the Z of D-348, and is the single source for the model and the BOM. The owner picks the beam section from a short stiffness script (Q&A). The model is a fresh copy of the parametric PrintNC V4 Metalcutter Fusion model, set by parameters first and changed directly only where a parameter cannot do it, built axis by axis through the Fusion MCP, each ending with a read-only check script (timeline health, body overlap, reach at the travel limits). The "tests" are script asserts and geometric checks.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (`tools/`, plain asserts, run with `PYTHONUTF8=1`); Fusion 360 API via `fusion_mcp_execute`; curl + Python for prices (CLAUDE.md).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-v5-printnc-design.md` (approved 26 Nov 2026; D-338 to D-349).

## Global Constraints
- Work area **400 x 250**; part accuracy ±0.02 mm.
- Moving gantry, fixed bed, bolted steel tube frame (PrintNC, D-341). **No welding.**
- **Only shop work: surface grinding, and only as the rail-seat fallback** (D-339, D-342). No milled, turned or laser-cut parts. Owner saws, drills and taps with printed drill guides.
- Beam: **taller steel tube standing up, both X rails on its front face** (D-349); section from Task 2.
- Table to nose **25 to 225**, Z travel 200 (D-348).
- Rack: **2 rows of 8 = 16 tools**, about 60 pitch, rows along X, **behind the bed**; holder tops **about 70 to 120 above the table**; longest tool up to about 150 below the nose (D-340, D-344, D-345, D-348).
- Motion: LiMo C5 DFU1605, HGR20 (D-136, D-343); inline motors on bought BK12/HM12 mounts; two Y screws (Y1, Y2) as PrintNC; Type 0 Z (NEMA 24 with brake) on bought cut tooling plate.
- Rail seats: epoxy self-levelling pour on the frame tubes; pass mark 0.02 mm, checked against HIWIN HG20 mounting tolerances before it is fixed.
- Budget: Phase 1 **£4,200**; flag any overrun, do not cut accuracy.
- Prices only from live pages, by curl, with the date; never estimate; no eBay; flag anything more than 10 % or £5 dearer.
- Ask the owner (Q&A tool) before any decision not in the spec; record decisions in `docs/decisions.md` from **D-350**.
- `Type_0_v3` and `Type_0_v4` are history: do not edit them. Do not save Fusion documents: the owner saves.
- `docs/bom.csv` is the source of truth (CRLF). Stop at about 130k tokens of context and hand over (CLAUDE.md).

## Review Focus
- A loaded spindle crossing the front row of the rack to reach the back row: tool tip + 5 must clear the front-row holder tops at the highest nose. Check in Task 6 with the longest tool.
- The gantry at Y max over the rack: the beam underside, Y rollers, Y motors and cable chain must clear the rack and its holders. Check in Task 6 at the Y limit.
- The two Y rails parallel and level after owner drilling: the drill guides and the pour must not lock in a twist between the two Y frame tubes. Check in Task 7 that the pour plan measures across both tubes, not only along each.
- The spindle at Y min reaching the bed's front edge with the gantry clear of the front frame tube and front screw supports. Check in Task 4 at the Y min limit.
- Chips off the bed onto the rack and the rear Y screw ends. Check in Task 6 that a guard or cover is in the model or listed in the BOM.

**Files:** Create `tools/v5_sizes.py`; create `docs/v5-sizes.md` (its printed table, one-line source per input).

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `sizes() -> dict` with keys `x_travel, y_travel, z_travel, nose_min, nose_max, rack_row_span, rack_y_depth, holder_top_min, holder_top_max, tool_max, x_rail, x_screw, y_rail, y_screw, z_rail, z_screw, beam_len, frame_x, frame_y` (mm), used by Tasks 2 to 8. Inputs as named constants at the top: work area, rack pitch 60, tools per row 8, row pitch in Y, holder stack 44 (nose at pickup below holder top, `docs/atc-wheel.md`), clear 5, HGH20CA block length and pitch, BK12/BF12 lengths, PrintNC roller and frame offsets.
