# EG mix test (cut down by D-160, 17 Nov 2026)

**D-160 (owner, 17 Nov 2026): all tests dropped except the mix bricks, and those are judged on surface and voids only.** Dropped: packing test, heat brick and pull test, beam section, rail-seat strip, stiffness prisms, expansion test (D-149). Background: `epoxy-options.md`, `curing-heat.md`.

**When:** build weeks 12–13 (22 Mar – 4 Apr 2027), indoors or in the heated shed, before the first casting.

## 1. Materials

| Part | Material | Density used |
|---|---|---|
| Coarse | GCL Winter Quartz 2–5 mm, washed, kiln-dried (F03) | 2.65 kg/L |
| Sand | Hancock & Brown kiln-dried pavior sand 0–1 mm (F04) | 2.65 |
| Filler | Valentine Clays whiting, d50 9 µm (F06) | 2.7 |
| Binder | Ampreg 30 resin + 3X Slow hardener, **100 : 26 by weight** (F01) | 1.10 (Ampreg 30 TDS, slow hardener, mixed) |
| Fallback | GCL quartz 1–3 mm, if the packing test wants it | 2.65 |

Everything is stored indoors for 48 h before use, so it is dry and at about 20 °C.

## 2. Starting recipes

Blends are by mass of the dry aggregate. The whiting stays at 10 % or less (its fine part raises the epoxy demand).

| Blend | Quartz 2–5 | Quartz 1–3 | Sand 0–1 | Whiting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 (baseline) | 50 % | – | 40 % | 10 % |
| P2 (coarser) | 55 % | – | 35 % | 10 % |
| P3 (finer) | 45 % | – | 45 % | 10 % |

**Blend P1 is used (D-160: no packing test).** P2, P3 and the 1–3 mm quartz fallback are kept only in case the bricks all fail.

What each epoxy level means. The packed stone must leave no more empty space than the epoxy can fill:

| Epoxy by mass | Epoxy by volume | Dry tamped density the blend must reach | Density of a void-free brick |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 % | 21.1 % | 2.09 kg/L | 2.32 kg/L |
| 11 % | 22.9 % | 2.04 kg/L | 2.29 kg/L |
| 12 % | 24.7 % | 1.99 kg/L | 2.27 kg/L |
| 13 % | 26.5 % | 1.95 kg/L | 2.24 kg/L |

For 1 kg of mix at 11 %: 110 g binder (87.3 g resin + 22.7 g hardener) and 890 g aggregate (P1: 445 g quartz, 356 g sand, 89 g whiting).

## 3. Mix bricks

| Piece | Size (mm) | EG mass | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1–A4 | 100 × 100 × 100 | 4 × 2.3 kg | Blend P1, epoxy at 10, 11, 12, 13 %; black pigment in A2 and A4 to set the amount (D-146) |

Moulds: sealed MDF or melamine offcuts, screwed, with silicone fillets. Release on the brick moulds as D-120 (A1 wax, A2 wax + PVA, A3 packing tape + wax, A4 your pick): it costs nothing extra, and the surfaces show which release to use.

## 4. Mixing and placing (the same method as the real castings)

1. Dry-mix the aggregate for the batch in the bucket with the paddle mixer (F17), about 1 minute. Dust mask on.
2. Weigh resin and hardener (100 : 26) to within 1 %. Stir 2–3 minutes, scraping the sides and bottom (datasheet: 2 minutes or more). Premix only: never add the parts to the stone separately (D-144).
3. Pour the binder onto the stone **straight away**. Never leave mixed resin standing in the bucket: in a mass it heats up and runs away.
4. Mix 3–4 minutes, until every stone is wet and there are no dry pockets. The datasheet gives no time for this step; lengthen it if the cut bricks show dry or resin-rich patches. At 10–12 % it looks like damp sand, not a liquid.
5. First, pack a thin layer of **fine mix** (the same recipe without the 2–5 mm quartz) against the mould floor, the inserts and under any flat bar. Press it in by hand (gloved).
6. Place the mix in **layers of 30–50 mm**. Tamp each layer with a flat-ended rod or a block, edges and corners first, until the surface closes and a sheen of resin shows. Rough up the top of each layer lightly before the next.
7. Note the time each batch was mixed and when it was placed.

Batch size for the real castings: about 10–12 kg per bucket. The base is 7–8 batches and about 3 layers; with one person mixing and one tamping, about 3 hours.

## 5. Schedule

| Session | Work | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make the 4 moulds and apply the releases | 2 |
| 2 | Cast A1–A4. Cure 48 h at 18 °C or more | 2 |
| 3 | Demould; look at the surfaces; saw each brick in half; **pick the epoxy level** | 1.5 |

## 6. How to judge

- **Surface:** outer faces smooth and closed, no bare stone, no pinholes or resin-starved patches; note how each release came off.
- **Voids:** saw each brick in half (X08). No void larger than 2 mm on the cut faces, no more than about 5 small ones on a 100 × 100 face; no resin layer thicker than 2 mm at the top and no band of bare coarse stone at the bottom.
- **Choose the lowest epoxy level that passes.** If none passes, tell a session (P2, P3 or the 1–3 mm fallback, section 2).

## 7. Quantities

The bricks use about 9.2 kg of EG: about 1.1 kg of binder over the four levels and about 8.1 kg of aggregate (P1: about 4.1 kg quartz 2–5, 3.3 kg sand, 0.8 kg whiting). The old plan's tests took about 4.3–4.7 kg of binder and 30 kg of aggregate, so the epoxy margin grows by about 3.3 kg; the castings figures are re-worked with the tub CAD.

## 8. Kit

Scales (0–5 kg to 1 g, 0–30 kg), buckets, mixing sticks, tamping rod, angle grinder with diamond disc (X08), PPE (X09: nitrile gloves, glasses, FFP3 mask). Epoxy sensitises skin: no bare-hand contact.

## 9. Record

One line per brick: date, epoxy %, pigment, batch masses, room and material temperature, humidity, time mixed and placed, release used, demould notes, and a photo of the outer faces and each cut face. Put the photos in the repo so a session can read them.

## Not tested any more (D-160): what is relied on instead

- Packing: blend P1 as written.
- Heat in a thick pour: the estimate in `curing-heat.md` only; the first tent run is on a real casting.
- Rail-bar bow and the neat-Ampreg rail seat: seen first on the real pour (D-148 levelling step stays).
- Stiffness and thermal expansion: not measured; the beam-assessment estimate (`beam-assessment.md`) stands.
